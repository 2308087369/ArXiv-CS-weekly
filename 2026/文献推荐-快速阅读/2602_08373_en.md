## 1. Introduction: Trust Crisis and Paradigm Shift in Embodied Planning

### 1.1 Weekly Abstract

The core task of embodied intelligence planners is to generate a sequence of actions executable in a physical environment based on high-level instructions (e.g., "clean up the spilled oil in the kitchen"). While traditional end-to-end large model planning excels in semantic coherence, it often suffers from "semantic hallucinations" or "safety blind spots" in scenarios involving complex safety constraints. For instance, a model might suggest mixing bleach and ammonia to clean the floor, which seems reasonable in a natural language context but would generate fatal chlorine gas in physical reality.

Existing safety mechanisms are mostly "passive gatekeeper" modes, using another set of LLMs as critics or hard-coded rules to intercept generated plans. This mode has two major limitations: first, probabilistic critics (LLM-as-a-Judge) still carry hallucination risks; second, simple Rejection cannot provide constructive feedback, causing agents to frequently fall into "refusal to execute" deadlocks under complex constraints, leading to low task success rates.

The proposal of **VIRF (Verifiable Iterative Refinement Framework)** marks a paradigm shift in safety mechanisms from "passive interception" to "active collaboration."

## 3. Key Mechanism: Traceable Axiom Synthesis and Knowledge Acquisition

The biggest obstacle to building formal verification systems is the "knowledge acquisition bottleneck," i.e., how to translate massive, ambiguous, and dynamic real-world safety guidelines into machine-readable logic axioms. VIRF solves this problem through a semi-automated, "human-in-the-loop" offline workflow.

  <img src="../../Resource/第六期/VIRF-The Traceable Axiom Synthesis workflow, a semi-automated, human-in-the-loop process for building the TBox from unstructured texts.png" width="90%" alt="Figure 2: Traceable Axiom Synthesis workflow">
  <br>
  <em>Figure 2: The Traceable Axiom Synthesis workflow. A semi-automated, human-in-the-loop TBox construction process.</em>
</div>
<br>

This workflow differs from RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) methods that randomly inject text at runtime; it is a rigorous process conducted during the design phase.
1.  **AI Synthesizer**: Retrieves relevant fragments from verified documents (e.g., safety manuals, chemical safety data sheets) and drafts OWL 2 logic axioms conforming to Manchester Syntax.
2.  **Human Arbiter**: Human experts act as arbiters, verifying "axiom-evidence pairs" to ensure logical rigor and authenticity.

Research data shows that this workflow synthesized 92 high-quality safety axioms in just two days, significantly expanding the agent's cognitive coverage of specific hazards (e.g., chemical mixing, child ingestion, hot oil splashing).

**Table 1: Comparative Analysis of VIRF Knowledge Acquisition Workflow vs. Traditional and Native LLM Methods**

| **Construction Speed** | Extremely Slow (Months/Years) | Real-time (Seconds) | Fast (Days) |
| **Logical Rigor** | Extremely High (Expert Assured) | Extremely Low (LLM Generated) | Extremely High (Human Audited) |

## 4. Perception and Reasoning: VLM Cascade and Parallel Verification

### 4.1 Three-Stage VLM Cascade Perception Pipeline

The effectiveness of formal reasoning relies heavily on the accuracy of its "symbolic grounding" in the current physical environment, i.e., the truthfulness of the ABox (assertion component). VIRF designed the VLM-Cascade perception pipeline to eliminate LLM hallucinations about environmental states.

The perception process is divided into three ordered stages:
1.  **Detect:** Determine objects in the scene and their bounding boxes.
2.  **Attribute-Refine:** Identify fine-grained states of objects (e.g., "is the stove on", "is the container sealed").
3.  **Relation-Refine:** Establish spatial and functional relationships between objects (e.g., "specific relationship between child and heat source").

This cascaded design embodies the "safety first" principle. Although perception latency reaches 168.4 seconds, far higher than the 85.2 seconds of ordinary detectors, its accuracy (76.3%) and ability to capture dangerous details are prerequisites for achieving a 0% Hazardous Action Rate (HAR). For safety-critical systems, the cost of missing a danger (False Negative) far outweighs the cost of increased latency.

### 4.2 Parallel Neuro-symbolic Reasoning Engine

After obtaining the perceived ABox and knowledge base TBox, VIRF initiates the iterative refinement cycle. The initial plan generated by the LLM Apprentice is converted into a series of logical queries to be verified. To address the computational burden of Description Logic Reasoning in long-sequence planning, VIRF adopts a multi-threaded parallel verification architecture.

The system does not check the plan sequentially from step 1 to N but creates parallel reasoning threads for each step. Each thread simulates the world state before execution and invokes the Pellet reasoner for verification. This architecture optimizes verification time from linear growth with action sequence length to constant-level time of the longest single-step reasoning (about 3 seconds), greatly enhancing the system's real-time response capability.

## 5. Interaction Mode: Dialogue Mechanism from "Reject" to "Teaching"

The core innovation of VIRF lies in its generated "causal pedagogical feedback." When the Logic Tutor detects a plan violating safety axioms, it not only points out which step is wrong but also explains the underlying logical conflict.

For example, when handling a chemical task, the Logic Tutor provides feedback in the following form:
> "Step 3 is unsafe. You are attempting to mix 'Cleaner A' and 'Cleaner B'. According to Safety Axiom SA-102, substances containing sodium hypochlorite mixed with acidic substances produce harmful chlorine gas. Scene perception shows 'Cleaner B' contains citric acid. Please modify the plan to avoid such mixing."

This deep causal information empowers the LLM Apprentice to perform "creative repair" rather than simple avoidance. Experiments prove that without causal feedback (i.e., only being told it is unsafe), the planner suffers a high False Negative Rate (FNR) of 33.0%, meaning the agent abandons tasks it could have completed because it fails to understand the root cause of the error.

## 6. Experimental Evaluation and Empirical Findings

Researchers conducted a series of rigorous evaluations of VIRF on a benchmark suite containing high-difficulty home safety tasks such as chemical mixing, hot oil handling, and child care.

### 6.1 Core Performance Metrics: HAR and GCR

The experiment focuses on two competing metrics: Hazardous Action Rate (HAR) and Goal-Condition Rate (GCR).

**Table 2: VIRF Performance Comparison in Complex Safety Tasks**

| Experimental Architecture Scheme | Hazardous Action Rate (HAR) | Goal-Condition Rate (GCR) | Repair Iterations (Mean) |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Native LLM (Zero-shot) | 38.5% | 52.1% | N/A |
| LLM + Static Rule Injection | 12.4% | 61.2% | N/A |
| **VIRF (Full Version)** | **0.0%** | **77.3%** | **1.1** |

Experimental results prove that VIRF achieved a perfect 0% HAR, completely eliminating unsafe behaviors, while its GCR (77.3%) significantly outperformed all baseline models. It required only 1.1 correction iterations on average to achieve safe and effective planning, demonstrating the efficiency of the pedagogical dialogue.

### 6.2 Ablation Studies

*   **VIRF-Reject** (Reject only without explanation): Led to a high false negative rate, proving the necessity of causal feedback.
*   **VIRF-Manual** (Manual common sense base): Unable to handle specialized safety scenarios (e.g., chemical identification).
*   **VIRF-RAG** (Retrieval Augmented): Resulted in 8.5% HAR due to knowledge pollution and randomness, supporting the superiority of "design-time axiom synthesis."

## 7. Discussion: Deep Evolution of Model Scale and Safety Mechanisms

### 7.1 Model Scale and Reasoning Capability Boundaries

*   **"Reasoning Floor" of 7B Models**: Experiments found that when using a 7B parameter model as the apprentice, VIRF's performance dropped significantly. Although the Logic Tutor provided precise feedback, the 7B model often failed to handle these logical constraints. This indicates that while neuro-symbolic frameworks can provide an "exoskeleton," the internal model must possess a certain baseline reasoning capability (Reasoning Threshold) to collaborate effectively.
*   **Equality Effect of 72B and Gemini-2.5**: In high-parameter model comparisons, the 72B open-source model supported by VIRF demonstrated safety comparable to Gemini-2.5. This suggests that through external logic anchors, slightly inferior open-source models can achieve or even exceed the performance of cutting-edge closed-source models in specific safety domains.

### 7.2 Second-Order Insights: Deep Evolution of Safety Mechanisms

Several deep trends regarding AI safety and autonomy evolution can be distilled from VIRF research:

1.  **"Unbalanced Game" of Perception and Reasoning**: VIRF accepted high latency in the perception layer (168.4s) in exchange for certainty in the reasoning layer. This foreshadows that future agent architectures may adopt a layered structure similar to human thinking: "System 1 (Fast Intuitive Response) and System 2 (Slow Logical Calibration)."
2.  **"Traceability" of Knowledge**: VIRF's Traceable Axiom Synthesis workflow links every Manchester syntax axiom back to its original safety document, perfectly aligning with regulatory trends like the EU AI Act requiring auditability.
3.  **Dialectical Unity of Safety and Creativity**: Precise causal feedback paradoxically stimulates the model's "compliant creativity." When the model knows the specific reason "mixing A and B is dangerous," it actively seeks alternative paths, thereby improving the overall task success rate.

## 8. Conclusion and Recommendation Summary

The VIRF framework is not just a technical innovation but a profound response to the limitations of large language models. It proves that building trustworthy, safe, and efficient embodied intelligence systems does not require abandoning the diversity of generative models, but rather equipping them with a "formal logic rudder."

Through axiom synthesis, cascaded perception, and pedagogical dialogue, VIRF achieved a 0% hazardous action rate and industry-leading goal completion rates in complex home safety tasks. This achievement lays a solid foundation for AI safety research in 2026 and beyond, heralding future agents that will not only be semantic "polymaths" but also verifiable, rigorous "actors" in the physical world.
